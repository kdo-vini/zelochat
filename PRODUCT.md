# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ZeloChat is used by owners and attendants of Brazilian lanchonetes and small food businesses. They work inside an operational dashboard while handling real WhatsApp conversations, order review, production status, catalog setup, delivery details, AI behavior, and billing.

Users are usually in a fast service context: desktop or notebook at the counter, sometimes mobile, with interruptions from customers, kitchen, delivery and payment confirmation. They need dense, predictable controls that make the next action obvious without exposing technical infrastructure.

## Product Purpose

ZeloChat organizes WhatsApp-native customer service for small Brazilian food businesses. It combines manual attendance, AI-assisted replies, catalog-aware order capture, ZeloMenu cart confirmation, production tracking, printing and subscription-gated access.

Success means the operator can trust the app during live service: messages arrive in the right conversation, AI automation respects business rules, orders do not duplicate, customer-facing copy is clear, and the shop can move from chat to production without retyping everything.

## Brand Personality

Practical, calm, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a serious operating tool for a small business, not a marketing page or a toy. Copy should be friendly Brazilian Portuguese, direct, and action-oriented.

## Anti-references

- Do not make operational screens look like landing pages, hero sections, or decorative SaaS dashboards.
- Do not expose provider names, endpoints, raw errors, IDs, webhooks, upstream failures, or internal service names in operator/customer-facing text.
- Do not use mock customer data in placeholders or empty states.
- Do not over-decorate task surfaces with large illustrations, gradients, or nested cards.
- Do not make ZeloMenu configuration imply access to ZeloPDV surfaces unless the commercial entitlement allows it.

## Design Principles

- Put the work first: primary actions, status, and operational risk should be visible without explanation-heavy UI.
- Preserve trust under pressure: failures need clear Brazilian Portuguese, next steps, and no raw technical detail.
- Keep surfaces dense but readable: lanchonete operators scan lists, drawers, modals, and queues repeatedly during service.
- Respect product boundaries: ZeloChat talks, ZeloMenu structures, ZeloPDV operates where entitled; shared infrastructure must not blur paid access.
- Use familiar controls: switches for binary publication states, inputs for editable values, icons for repeated tool actions, and dialogs only when the workflow needs them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation and visible focus on interactive elements. Avoid relying on color alone for status. Keep motion short and state-driven, and respect reduced-motion preferences. All customer/operator-facing text should be Brazilian Portuguese.
